# StagePilot – 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

Follow this script for the live presentation to hackathon judges.

---

## Preparation (Before Pitch)
1. Open **two side-by-side browser windows**:
   - **Left Window:** Organizer War-Room (`http://localhost:5173/organizer`)
   - **Right Window:** Anchor Teleprompter (`http://localhost:5173/anchor`)
2. Log in using the 1-click demo buttons:
   - Left: **Organizer Demo** (`organizer@stagepilot.io`)
   - Right: **Anchor Demo** (`anchor@stagepilot.io`)

---

## Act 1: The Problem (0:00 – 0:45)
- **Speaker:**
  > *"Live event stages are chaotic. Speakers run late, VIPs show up unannounced, and schedule changes are scribbled on sticky notes or yelled across noisy backstage radios. The stage anchor is often the last person to know when things change."*
- **Action:** Point to both screens showing synchronized `Global Tech Horizon Summit 2026`.

---

## Act 2: The Cascading Delay Engine (0:45 – 1:30)
- **Speaker:**
  > *"Watch what happens when our keynote speaker encounters a 10-minute technical delay. In traditional events, the schedule is ruined."*
- **Action:**
  1. On the **Organizer Dashboard** (Left Window), click **+ Delay** on Session 1.
  2. Select **+10 mins** and click **Cascade +10m Delay**.
- **Judge Impact:**
  - Notice the **Right Window (Anchor View)** immediately rings a soft alert chime.
  - A sticky notice pops up: `"+10 MINS – Schedule Delay Broadcast"`.
  - The countdown timer automatically shifts without refreshing the page!
  - The Event Health Card updates to **Running Late (+10m)**.

---

## Act 3: Contextual AI Scripts & Teleprompter (1:30 – 2:15)
- **Speaker:**
  > *"Now the anchor needs to fill that 10-minute gap. Instead of panicking, StagePilot's AI generates a tailored delay script."*
- **Action:**
  1. On Anchor View, click **AI Scripts** -> **Delay Filler**.
  2. Choose **Lighthearted Tone** and click **Generate**.
  3. Click **Full Prompter**.
  4. Demonstrate **Auto-Scroll** and **Mirror Mode** (for stage teleprompter glass).

---

## Act 4: Emergency Broadcast & Co-Pilot (2:15 – 2:45)
- **Speaker:**
  > *"What if an emergency backstage direction happens?"*
- **Action:**
  1. On Organizer Dashboard, click **Broadcast Alert**.
  2. Choose **"VIP Guest arrived early, prioritize stage entry"** (Critical).
  3. Send broadcast.
  4. The Anchor's screen flashes a glowing red alert banner immediately.
  5. Open **AI Co-Pilot drawer** and ask: *"Give me an icebreaker question"*. The Co-Pilot responds in under 1 second.

---

## Act 5: Conclusion (2:45 – 3:00)
- **Speaker:**
  > *"StagePilot turns live event chaos into flawless, synchronized production. Thank you!"*
